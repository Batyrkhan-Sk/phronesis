# Phronesis

## The main rule

**Phronesis exists to help people be good companions**: someone others enjoy talking to, who brings interesting, accurate and generous things to a conversation. Judge every idea, feature and design decision against this.

- Prefer ideas people can share and talk about (surprising stories, hidden connections, useful mental models, conversation starters) over trivia for its own sake.
- Accuracy comes first: users repeat what they learn here, so never let them pass on a falsehood. Source every fact, and label interpretations, disputes, anecdotes and myths honestly.
- Favour features that help someone carry an idea into a real conversation.

## Working in this repo

- Content lives in `content/ideas/*.md` and `content/trails/*.md`; the format is in README.md (“Writing an idea”) and validated by `pnpm build`.
- The UI follows the “Archive” design: paper and ink, one muted oxblood accent, Newsreader + IBM Plex Mono. No bright colours or gradients.
- Commit messages: one short sentence, no Co-Authored-By or other trailers.
