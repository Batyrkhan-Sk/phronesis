We tend to put “Ancient Egypt” in one mental box, with pharaohs, pyramids and Cleopatra all together. But Egyptian civilisation lasted for **about 3,000 years**.

| Event | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Great Pyramid built | c. 2560 BC |
| Cleopatra dies | 30 BC |
| Apollo 11 lands on the Moon | 1969 AD |

Pyramid → Cleopatra: **about 2,530 years**. Cleopatra → Moon landing: **about 2,000 years**.

Cleopatra was also **Greek-Macedonian** by descent, the last ruler of the Ptolemaic dynasty founded by one of Alexander the Great’s generals. To her the pyramids were ancient monuments, as old to her as the Roman Empire is to us.

## Other timelines that feel wrong

- **Woolly mammoths** were still alive on Wrangel Island (Arctic Siberia) around 2000 BC, after the pyramids were built.
- **Tyrannosaurus rex** lived closer in time to us than to *Stegosaurus*.
- The **University of Oxford** was teaching before the Aztec capital was founded.

These comparisons are useful because they correct a common mistake: we **compress** the distant past and stretch the recent past.
