Draw circles around the three nearest specks of land, Ducie Island (Pitcairn Islands), Motu Nui (near Easter Island) and Maher Island (Antarctica), and they meet at one point in the South Pacific: **48°52.6′S 123°23.6′W**. That is **Point Nemo**, about **2,688 km** from the nearest shore.

The location was calculated in **1992** by the Croatian-Canadian survey engineer **Hrvoje Lukatela** using computer geometry. It was named after **Captain Nemo** from Jules Verne’s *Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Seas*. *Nemo* is Latin for **“no one”**.

## Closer to space than to land

The International Space Station orbits at about **400 km**. When it passes over, the astronauts are the **nearest humans** to Point Nemo by a wide margin.

## The spacecraft cemetery

Because nobody lives nearby and few ships pass, space agencies aim falling spacecraft at the surrounding region. Hundreds of objects have been deliberately sunk there, including the **Mir** space station in 2001. The ISS is planned to follow at the end of its life.

H. P. Lovecraft placed the sunken city of R’lyeh, where Cthulhu lies dreaming, in the same remote stretch of ocean in 1928, decades before anyone calculated the point.
